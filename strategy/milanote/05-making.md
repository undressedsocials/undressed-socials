# 05 · MAKING

## Purpose

### convert
prospect thinks: **"they could actually do this for us."**

MAKING is not "we know why this works." that is THINKING.
MAKING is **"here is what we would actually do."**

one word for the whole layer: **translation.**

### the question before posting
**can someone see exactly how undressed would turn an insight into work?**

if the post ends on an insight, it's in the wrong layer. MAKING ends on something you
could brief: a shot, a casting note, a content series, a rule, a drop.

### why this layer sells without selling
we demonstrate the service instead of describing it. no rate card, no "book a call" on
every slide. a CMO transposes it onto their own business by themselves — which is the
entire mechanism.

## Sub-pillars

### IF IT WERE OURS
take a brand, category or fictional product and pitch it without being hired.
not a redesign. a **world-building** exercise. full spec on board `06`.

### ONE PRODUCT, THREE WORLDS
one boring product, three completely different worlds. the live demonstration of the
strategy process. full spec on board `06`.

### from insight to execution
the bridge format — takes a THINKING post and finishes the job. full spec on board `06`.

### the work
real client work, shown as a thinking chain rather than a reveal.

### process / the undress
how the strategic groundwork actually runs. the only place where the offer is explicit.

## Worked examples

### if this water brand were ours
**product:** water.

**don't build around:** purity. mountains. hydration. blue gradients.

**build around:** sunday mornings · wet hair · tennis clubs · hotel beds · white shirts ·
late lunches · 6am bakeries · the mediterranean in september

**the social executions:**
1. editorial shoot — bottles sweating beside wet swimsuits
2. creator series — *"where were you at 11:43 on sunday?"*
3. still-life series — the bottle photographed inside bags, cars, hotel rooms
4. community — the best places people have drunk it
5. drop — a limited bottle designed with a swimwear brand

now someone can literally imagine hiring us.

### if this bank were ours
we wouldn't make another financial-literacy carousel.
we'd position financial confidence like modern luxury, and build social on **access**,
not interest rates:

- **the key** — access to places
- **the table** — dinners with interesting people
- **the index** — things worth spending on
- **the receipt** — people's best €100 purchase

finance becomes culturally interesting. that is the kind of thinking that makes a CMO stop.

### if this hotel were ours
the hotel isn't the content. **the life around the hotel is.**
people, rituals, the local scene, music, objects, food, after dark. rooms appear
maybe one post in eight, and never as the hero.

### one product, three worlds — sparkling water
**world 01 · tennis club** — white cotton, clay, sweat, membership, summer
**world 02 · nightlife** — chrome, flash, bathrooms, 2:14am, the taxi
**world 03 · design** — glass, architecture, editorial still life, objects
> same product. completely different desire.

### from insight to execution — beauty × food
**slide 1** — beauty is borrowing from food.
**slide 2** — why: sensory appeal, at the moment screens got flatter.
**slide 3** — so what: people want to feel texture through a screen.
**slide 4** — if we were building a skincare campaign tomorrow:
then three actual shots. cream on stone fruit. skin macro at breakfast-table light.
a melting texture held in the hand, flash on.

## The work — how to show it

### never "new work for client X"
show the thinking chain instead:

**the brief** — "make product X relevant to younger consumers."
↓
**the insight** — the category talks almost entirely about function.
↓
**the world** — we borrowed codes from nightlife.
↓
**the creative rule** — never photograph the product before sunset.
↓
**the output** — the campaign.

the creative rule is the slide people screenshot. always include one.

### BTS, but not "behind the scenes from today's shoot 😊"
> 11:37. everything we planned just became useless.

then the unexpected moment on set that became the hero shot.

> the image we almost didn't shoot.

### team, but not "meet the team"
> three people argued about the shade of a napkin for 22 minutes. here's why.

then explain art direction. the argument *is* the service.

### process, but not "our process"
> the shoot starts three weeks before the camera comes out.

strategy · casting · location · styling · references · shot list · production.
which is, not coincidentally, exactly what we sell.

### the undress
the one post where the offer is explicit.
> before we make anything, we take everything off.

what it is, why it's mandatory, what comes out of it. it only works because the posts
before it earned the worldview — see board `10`, post 12.

## Cadence note

### two per twenty, minimum, on real work
MAKING can't be all hypotheticals. IF IT WERE OURS proves imagination; client work proves
delivery. a feed with no real work reads as a very talented student. keep at least two
real-work posts in every twenty, even when the work is small.
