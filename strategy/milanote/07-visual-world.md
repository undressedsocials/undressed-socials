# 07 · Visual World

## The territory

### editorial appetite
not classic luxury. not gen-z maximalism. not clean scandinavian agency design.

**fashion × food × tactility × humour × ordinary objects × sensuality × imperfection**

the common denominator across every reference on this board isn't "fashion."
it's **appetite**.

### the thing that repeats in almost every image
**something familiar has been displaced.**

lemon → hair roller. tomato → shoe accessory. sneaker → plated dinner.
orange → fashion prop. laundromat → editorial set. cherry → art object.

that is not a coincidence and it is not just a mood. it is conceptually identical to what
we do with brands: *take something familiar and put it somewhere unexpected until people
see it differently.*

**displacement is the visual philosophy of undressed.** everything below is a variation on it.

## The six principles

references are filed by principle, not by vibe. a reference with no principle is
decoration and doesn't belong on the board.

### 01 · displacement
the object is in the wrong place, doing the wrong job, and it works.
**use for:** DESIRE category collisions, ONE PRODUCT THREE WORLDS.
**the test:** can you name the rule that was broken in three words?

### 02 · appetite & texture
gloss, cream, citrus, condensation, skin, fabric weight. things you can feel through a screen.
**use for:** DESIRE, and every beauty/food/hospitality argument in THINKING.
**the test:** would you touch it if it were in front of you?

### 03 · the frame that hides
the face is covered, cropped, obscured, turned away. the gesture carries the image.
**use for:** anything where the subject is the *behaviour*, not the person. also solves
casting budget and usage rights.
**the test:** is the image stronger because you can't see who it is?

### 04 · ordinary, made editorial
a laundromat, a carpet, a bathroom floor, a bare concrete room, a receipt — lit and framed
like a campaign.
**use for:** the cheapest, most on-brand content we make. `the product didn't change. the context did.`
**the test:** would this location normally be cropped out?

### 05 · colour as the loudest thing
one saturated colour doing all the shouting. usually against a flat field.
**use for:** feed rhythm. this is what stops the grid going beige between the quiet posts.
**the test:** does it survive at thumbnail size with no type?

### 06 · imperfection, art-directed
flash, grain, motion, an odd crop, a snapshot angle. deliberately made, never accidental.
**use for:** BTS, the raw mode, and every "brands are getting less polished" argument.
**the test:** could you brief it? if not, it's just a bad photo.

## Reference audit — the existing library

the twelve references already in the system (`assets/mood/m01`–`m12`), regrouped.
each gets a primary principle, what it proves, and where it gets used.

### m01 · magazine over the face, timber room
**principles:** 03 the frame that hides · 05 colour (that orange-red is `--flame`)
**proves:** the product being sold can be the thing that hides the person. the spread reads
"expect the unexpected" while literally covering her face — the joke and the craft in one frame.
**use for:** DESIRE `the frame that hides` · any UNDRESSED about brands hiding behind polish.

### m02 · headscarf, shades, acid-yellow tiled wall
**principles:** 05 colour · 03 the frame that hides
**proves:** one wall of `--sun` yellow does more than any styling decision in the frame.
the strongest colour-system match in the whole library — this is blood · sun made photographic.
**use for:** DESIRE stop-power · the reference for any brand-colour argument.

### m03 · a mountain of whipped butter with daisies, held out on silver
**principles:** 02 appetite & texture · 01 displacement
**proves:** the single most "editorial appetite" image we own. food behaving like sculpture,
served like a gift, shot like a portrait.
**use for:** the hero image for `why everything suddenly looks edible` · WE WANT TO EAT THIS IMAGE.

### m04 · a silver nike rift plated for dinner, knife and fork
**principles:** 01 displacement · 02 appetite
**proves:** the thesis image of the entire account. a product placed in a completely
foreign ritual — and it makes you want the shoe more, not less. *products belong to
categories. brands belong to worlds*, in one photograph.
**use for:** the manifesto post (board `10`, post 01) · BORROWED CODES cover · pin it.

### m05 · head-first into a laundromat dryer, orange heels
**principles:** 04 ordinary made editorial · 01 displacement · 05 colour
**proves:** the location nobody would choose, plus one saturated colour, equals a fashion
image. also `the frame that hides` — no face at all, and it loses nothing.
**use for:** `the product didn't change. the context did.` · IF IT WERE OURS for any
unglamorous category.

### m06 · reading in bed, colour-blocked linen, feet up the wall
**principles:** 05 colour · 06 imperfection
**proves:** colour used as composition rather than decoration — green, pink, orange, blue,
periwinkle all in one frame and it still reads calm. closest reference to the five-pair system.
**use for:** the colour-system reference · anything about a recognisable world holding
different content types.

### m07 · a red dj controller on the lap, jeans down, tiled bathroom
**principles:** 04 ordinary made editorial · 05 colour · 06 imperfection
**proves:** humour without meme culture. a bathroom floor, an undignified pose, one perfect
red object — and it reads as fashion, not a joke.
**use for:** BORROWED CODES `what can X steal from nightlife` · proof that funny and
beautiful are not opposites.

### m08 · a vintage phone on carpet, hands only, film grain
**principles:** 03 the frame that hides · 06 imperfection
**proves:** enormous negative space, no face, an obsolete object, and it's the most
expensive-looking image in the set. restraint as a luxury code.
**use for:** `why some brands feel expensive before you see the price` · quiet posts
between loud ones.

### m09 · working on the floor of a raw building, laptop, notes, overhead
**principles:** 04 ordinary made editorial · 06 imperfection
**proves:** the only reference showing *work* rather than product — and it still isn't a
"team photo." the correct model for BTS.
**use for:** the work / process posts · `the shoot starts three weeks before the camera
comes out`.

### m10 · a cartwheel in a green field, red rubber boots
**principles:** 06 imperfection (motion) · 05 colour · 01 displacement
**proves:** movement caught mid-fall, and the boots are the wrong footwear for everything
about the image. joy without cuteness.
**use for:** reel covers · anything about brands taking a risk · the `--blood` on green pairing.

### m11 · yellow socks and sandals, oranges spilling from a plastic bag on steps
**principles:** 01 displacement · 02 appetite · 05 colour
**proves:** groceries as a styling element. the clear plastic bag is doing the work —
the cheapest possible prop, art-directed.
**use for:** `make groceries behave like luxury goods` · the fruit × fashion chain.

### m12 · an older man on a beach, pink tee, strawberry milkshake, direct flash
**principles:** 06 imperfection (flash) · 02 appetite · 05 colour
**proves:** casting against type. the most commercial image in the set, and it works
entirely because of who is holding the bottle.
**use for:** anything about casting · IF IT WERE OURS for a beverage · the argument that
casting beats budget.

### what the library is missing
gaps to fill on the next production day, in priority order:
1. **texture macro** — nothing in the set is shot close enough to feel. one cream, one
   fabric, one condensation.
2. **a genuine still life** — every reference has a body in it. we need objects alone,
   lit hard, on seamless. that is the mode most client work will actually need.
3. **type-in-the-world** — signage, packaging, a printed page. m01 is the only one and it's borrowed.
4. **night** — the whole library is daylight. nightlife is a world we intend to borrow from
   constantly and we can't currently show it.

## The four visual modes

### don't build a fixed agency feed
no single template. red background, yellow type, same border, same carousel, forever, is
wallpaper — and it flattens exactly the taste we're trying to prove.

the `--blood` / `--sun` system is **punctuation, not wallpaper**.

### mode A · editorial
full-bleed image or video. barely branded. no type, or one line.
**used by:** most of DESIRE, world panels in ONE PRODUCT THREE WORLDS.

### mode B · thought
type, imagery and simple diagrams. the most recognisably undressed mode — this is where
gluten black, the pairs and the wordmark live.
**used by:** all of THINKING, the argument slides in MAKING.

### mode C · intervention
collage, objects, unexpected pairings, scribbles, annotations over images.
**used by:** IF IT WERE OURS, creative experiments, taste archive.

### mode D · raw
camera roll, screenshots, a voice note waveform, a whatsapp thread, a sketch, a shot list.
**used by:** the work, BTS, process.

### why four
together they read as **one person's taste**, not one canva template. the consistency
comes from the eye and the colour pairs, not from the layout.

**rhythm rule:** never three of the same mode in a row in the grid.

## Colour

### the five pairs, unchanged
always colour on colour. one family per piece. never a rainbow.

| pair | surface | text | use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | blood `#87000C` | sun `#F4ED57` | the default. manifesto, UNDRESSED, anything loud. |
| 2 | sky `#C6DCFF` | blood `#87000C` | the calm one. process, the undress, quieter thinking. |
| 3 | candy `#FBCDE0` | flame `#E64B27` | appetite, beauty, food, humour. |
| 4 | olive `#58621E` | blush `#FFEAF2` | the grown-up one. case studies, real work. |
| 5 | teal `#1E5B62` | mint `#E9FFC1` | night, nightlife, anything cool-toned. |

### how colour maps to series
one pair per series, held for at least six months, so the grid becomes readable by colour
before anyone reads a word:
**BORROWED CODES** → pair 1 · **UNDRESSED** → pair 1 · **IF IT WERE OURS** → pair 3 ·
**ONE PRODUCT THREE WORLDS** → pair 5 · **the work** → pair 4 · **process** → pair 2.

## What we never shoot

- stock-library gestures — the handshake, the laptop-and-coffee, the pointing at a screen
- a team photo lined up against a wall
- a phone held up showing the feed we made
- perfectly symmetrical flat lays with props at 45°
- anything where the only idea is the colour grade
- a quote card that would work equally well for any agency
