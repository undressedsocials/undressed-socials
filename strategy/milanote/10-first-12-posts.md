# 10 · First 12 Posts

## Launch logic

### don't introduce the company
the instinct is post 01 = "hi, we're undressed, here's what we do." resist it.

**start with a belief.** the company gets introduced at post 12, and by then the worldview
has already been proved eleven times — so the offer lands as a conclusion rather than a pitch.

### the arc
posts 01–05 establish taste and thesis. 06–09 prove thinking and production.
10–11 make the reader uncomfortable about their own brand. 12 answers it.

### pacing
three a week, four weeks. don't accelerate. the first twelve are the argument — if one
isn't ready, hold the slot rather than filling it.

### the rule for all twelve
no "link in bio" until post 12.

## The twelve

### 01 · manifesto — YOUR PRODUCT IS NOT YOUR WORLD
**layer:** THINKING · **mode:** B · **pair:** 1 blood · sun
**viewer should think:** *oh — that's a real position.*
**carousel:** 1 the line, large · 2 a product is: water. skincare. a hotel room. · 3 a world is: after-hours tennis. japanese bathing. 6am bakeries. · 4 products belong to categories. brands belong to worlds. · 5 our job isn't to fill your category's feed. it's to find the world your brand deserves. · 6 *we make brands feel wanted.*
**caption:** the thesis, three sentences. no CTA.
**note:** m04 (the plated sneaker) is the cover.

### 02 · desire — one incredible image
**layer:** DESIRE · **mode:** A
**viewer should think:** *they have unusually good taste.*
> make ordinary things hard to ignore.

no teaching. no explanation. the deliberate silence after post 01 is the point.

### 03 · UNDRESSED 01 — why some brands feel expensive before you see the price
**layer:** THINKING · **mode:** B · **pair:** 1
**viewer should think:** *they notice things I hadn't named.*
what we see: restraint, space, slow pacing, few words → why: scarcity of information reads
as confidence → what it means: your price is communicated by everything except the price.

### 04 · BORROWED CODES 01 — what food can steal from fashion
**layer:** THINKING · **mode:** B · **pair:** 1
**viewer should think:** *I've never thought about my category like that.*
not the observation — the **execution**. casting. drops. editorial photography.
collaborations. seasonality. slide 5 is what it looks like on social, and it's the one
that separates us from every account that has made this observation already.

### 05 · desire — fashion / fruit / strange object
**layer:** DESIRE · **mode:** A
> taste doesn't know categories.

reference: m11 (oranges, yellow socks, steps) or shoot the raspberry heel.

### 06 · IF IT WERE OURS 01 — insurance
**layer:** MAKING · **mode:** C · **pair:** 3 candy · flame
**viewer should think:** *I wonder what they'd do with us.*
> if insurance were ours, we'd stop trying to make insurance interesting.

pick the most boring category on purpose. that is the whole flex: if we can build a world
around insurance, the reader's category is not the problem they thought it was.

### 07 · interpreted culture
**layer:** THINKING · **mode:** B
**viewer should think:** *they see things before other agencies.*
> everyone is copying X. here's what they're actually responding to.

whatever is genuinely current in launch week. one shift, named, with the third beat.
if nothing qualifies under the 48-hour rule, swap in T07 *attention is rented.*

### 08 · philosophy — attention is rented. recognition is owned.
**layer:** THINKING · **mode:** B · **pair:** 2 sky · blood
**viewer should think:** *they're not selling me virality.*
why a viral post and a brand are different assets, and why most agencies sell the first
because it's easier to invoice.

### 09 · production — what the final image doesn't show
**layer:** MAKING · **mode:** D raw
**viewer should think:** *they can actually make things.*
the mess two centimetres outside the frame. first proof of production capability, and the
first time the feed goes deliberately ugly.

### 10 · BORROWED CODES 02 — what hospitality could steal from music
**layer:** THINKING · **mode:** B · **pair:** 1
line-ups. anticipation. the aftermovie. tiers of access. the year-round calendar between events.
by the second one, the series is recognisable — which is the point of running it this early.

### 11 · UNDRESSED 02 — you probably don't need more content
**layer:** THINKING → MAKING · **mode:** B
**viewer should think:** *…that might be us.*
the discomfort post. when the real problem is positioning, visual language, world or
distribution — not volume. ends on the four things to fix instead.

this tees up post 12 completely. it is the most important post of the twelve.

### 12 · the undress
**layer:** MAKING · **mode:** B → D · **pair:** 2 sky · blood
**viewer should think:** *I want them to tell me what my brand could become.*
> before we make anything, we take everything off.

what the undress actually is, why nothing starts without it, what comes out of it.
the first explicit offer in a month of posting — and it lands differently because the
previous eleven posts already proved the worldview.

**caption:** ends with the invitation. first "link in bio" of the account.

## Pin these three

### 1 · what we believe
post 01. product → world → desire.

### 2 · how we think
post 04 or post 11. the cultural translation, in action.

### 3 · what we do
post 12. the undress, and what follows it.

someone should understand the whole company from those three, without scrolling further.

## After the twelve

### week 5 onward
switch to the mix on board `02` — 6 DESIRE, 8 THINKING, 6 MAKING per 20 — and start
planning in **chains** rather than individual posts.

### the first chain
fruit × fashion, as specced on board `02`. it uses the references already in the library
(m03, m11, m12) so it can run at low production cost while the shoot budget builds.

### the first review
at post 24, not post 12. twelve posts is not enough data and reviewing early leads to
abandoning the position before it has been seen. what to look at:
- which posts got **saved** and **sent**, not liked
- did anyone quote the language back at us
- did anyone ask "what would you do with my brand" unprompted — that is the only KPI
  that matters in the first quarter
