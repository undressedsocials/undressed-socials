# 09 · Content Bank

## How to use this board

### the queue, not the calendar
50 ideas, tagged by layer, series and effort. pull 8–10 at the start of each month,
check the mix against board `02`, then schedule.

exception to the file convention: the three bank columns below are **tables**, not
individual notes. paste each as one note to start. once you're working in it, split the
rows into separate notes so they can be dragged into a monthly board.

### effort key
| tag | means |
| --- | --- |
| **E** | easy — an hour. curation, a type card, a repost with a point of view. |
| **M** | medium — half a day. a carousel that needs writing and layout. |
| **P** | production — a shoot, a set build, a proper edit. **one per month, maximum.** |

### the standing balance
never let the queue drop below 12 unmade ideas, and never let it be more than a third **P**.
a bank full of production ideas is a bank that produces nothing.

## Maintenance

### monthly harvest
after each THINKING post ships, write its MAKING answer straight into the bank —
*"so what would we actually make?"* — before you move on. that is where next month's
MAKING column comes from, and it costs ten minutes while the thinking is still warm.

### quarterly cull
anything sitting in the bank unmade for two quarters gets deleted, not carried.
if it wasn't worth making in six months, it was never an idea — it was a topic.
