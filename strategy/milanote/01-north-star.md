# 01 · North Star

## The belief

### products belong to categories. brands belong to worlds.
the whole company in one sentence.

a product is: water. skincare. a hotel room. a bank account.
a world is: after-hours tennis. japanese bathing culture. a members' club. 6am bakeries.

our job is not to fill your category's feed.
it is to work out which world your brand deserves to live in.

the sales effect is the point: the prospect starts asking *"what world does my brand
belong to?"* — and then *"I don't know. I want them to figure it out."*

### we make brands feel wanted.
the line that sits **underneath** `we strip brands bare. then we make them loud.`

the existing line says what we do. this one says why it matters. it goes in captions,
the bio, and the closing slide of manifesto carousels — not on the homepage hero.

everything connects to it:
- **strategy** — to know what should be wanted
- **creative direction** — to make it look wanted
- **social** — so people encounter and want it repeatedly
- **creators** — to make the world socially believable
- **culture** — because desire never happens in a vacuum

### the question under every post
**why does this make us want something?**

that is the invisible editorial filter. it is what lets us post about fashion, food,
hospitality, music, packaging, architecture, sport, subcultures and objects without
becoming a lifestyle magazine.

## Editorial territory

### culture → desire → social execution
that arrow is the territory. all three parts, in that order.

others stop at culture → consumer behaviour. explaining the shift is the commodity now.
we take the next step every time: *so what does that brand actually photograph? who does
it cast? where does it appear? how does it talk? what does its instagram look like?*

translation is the moat. not observation.

### what we are a publication about
desirability. taste. the cultural codes behind brands people want.
run by a social media studio, which is why we can also build the thing.

### what we are not
- not a social media news account
- not a tips account
- not a portfolio
- not a moodboard account
- not marketing-guru linkedin

any of those is achievable in a month and worth nothing.

## The filter

### the five questions
run every post through these before it is scheduled.

1. **could another social media agency have posted this?** → if yes, don't.
2. **would I still care if the undressed logo disappeared?**
3. **does it show taste, or claim we have taste?**
4. **is there an observation here that makes someone think differently?**
5. **could a brand owner imagine their company becoming more interesting because of us?**

these five kill roughly half of what an agency would normally produce. good.

### the harshest one
> if we removed the logo, would this still feel like something only undressed would make or say?

if not, keep working.

## What the funnel should feel like

### after 1 post
they're interesting.

### after 3 posts
they have unusually good taste.

### after 6 posts
they understand why brands become culturally relevant.

### after 10 posts
they could actually turn that thinking into campaigns.

### after the website
I want them to tell me what my brand could become.

that is the funnel. not post → CTA → book a call, every single time.
