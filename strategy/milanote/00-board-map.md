# 00 · Board map

The ten boards, in build order. Create these as sub-boards inside `moodporn undressed`.

## Structure

### 01 · north star
what undressed believes. the two lines. the editorial territory. the five-question filter.
read before anything else. changes maybe twice a year.

### 02 · content architecture
DESIRE / THINKING / MAKING. the master table, the mix, the chains, the one-line brief.
the board you check when you are unsure where a post belongs.

### 03 · DESIRE
stop-power. taste without explanation. ~30% of the feed.

### 04 · THINKING
observation, interpretation, implication. ~40% of the feed.

### 05 · MAKING
translation into work. ~30% of the feed. the layer that converts.

### 06 · recurring series
the five named formats, each with a fixed structure so they are recognisable on sight.

### 07 · visual world
editorial appetite. six visual principles. the existing references regrouped by principle,
not by vibe. four visual modes. the colour system.

### 08 · tone of voice
what we sound like. what we never sound like. approved headlines. banned phrases.
caption architecture. the german rule.

### 09 · content bank
50 concrete post ideas, tagged by layer, series and effort. the working queue.

### 10 · first 12 posts
the exact launch sequence. what each post is for. what it should make the viewer think.

## Working rhythm

### monthly
pull 8–10 from `09 content bank`. check the mix against `02`. assign effort.
one production-heavy piece maximum per month.

### weekly
write from the brief on `02`. run the five questions from `01` before scheduling.

### quarterly
harvest: every THINKING post that performed gets a MAKING post built from it.
that is the discipline that keeps the feed commercial.
