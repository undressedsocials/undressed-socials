# 02 · Content Architecture

## The master table

### DESIRE / THINKING / MAKING
the only three buckets. everything else is a series or a format sitting inside one of them.

| | **DESIRE** | **THINKING** | **MAKING** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **prospect thinks** | "fuck, they have taste." | "they understand brands differently." | "they could actually do this for us." |
| **purpose** | STOP | CONVINCE | CONVERT |
| **audience feeling** | appetite | recognition, slight discomfort | confidence |
| **the question** | can this stop someone before they understand it? | does this give an observation they hadn't considered? | can they see exactly how we'd turn an insight into work? |
| **post formula** | familiar thing + unexpected world | observation → why → what changed → implication | problem → cultural insight → the move → execution |
| **series inside** | visual desire, category collisions, taste archive, creative experiments | BORROWED CODES, UNDRESSED, cultural observations, interpreted news | IF IT WERE OURS, ONE PRODUCT THREE WORLDS, INSIGHT → EXECUTION, the work |
| **share of feed** | 30% | 40% | 30% |

### the sequence in the viewer's head
**I want to look → I want to listen → I want them to do this for my brand.**

### the line that separates THINKING from MAKING
this is the one that gets blurred most often.

- "brands are doing X because Y changed" → **THINKING**
- "because Y changed, here is what we would actually do" → **MAKING**

if the post ends on an insight, it is THINKING. if it ends on a shot list, a casting
brief, a content series or a campaign idea, it is MAKING.

## The mix

### per 20 posts
| layer | posts |
| --- | ---: |
| DESIRE — visual world | 6 |
| THINKING — BORROWED CODES | 4 |
| THINKING — UNDRESSED / news | 4 |
| MAKING — IF IT WERE OURS | 3 |
| MAKING — work, proof, process | 3 |

### why the ratio is the strategy
70% educational → a marketing education account.
70% aesthetic → pinterest.
70% work → a portfolio.

none of those sell a strategy-first studio. the mix does.

### what is NOT a pillar
tips. trends. news. inspiration. BTS. case studies.

those are **formats**. they are how a post is made, not what it is for. every one of them
has to sit inside DESIRE, THINKING or MAKING, and answer that layer's question. this is
the single rule that keeps the feed coherent while the subjects stay wildly different.

## Chains

### never plan by weekday
monday = desire, wednesday = thinking, friday = making is mechanical and it shows.

plan in **chains**: one subject, carried across all three layers, over one to two weeks.
three posts stop being three posts and become one argument.

### chain example — fruit × fashion
**01 · DESIRE** — tomatoes inside heels. caption: *fruit belongs everywhere.*
**02 · THINKING** — why food keeps entering fashion imagery. sensuality, tactility, colour, recognisability.
**03 · MAKING** — if we were building a beverage campaign on that shift: fruit jewellery, fashion casting, wet still lifes, food styling, one creator concept.

### chain example — hotels
**01 · DESIRE** — a room-service trolley shot like a campaign. *room service, but make it editorial.*
**02 · THINKING** — why hotels are becoming media brands. music, retail, food, creators, events, publishing.
**03 · MAKING** — if this boutique hotel were ours, its instagram wouldn't be about rooms. people, rituals, the local scene, objects, after dark.

### chain example — a bank
**01 · DESIRE** — a card photographed like jewellery. no financial explanation at all.
**02 · THINKING** — financial brands could learn more from members' clubs than from other banks. access, recognition, belonging.
**03 · MAKING** — if this bank were ours, social would be built on access, not interest rates. *the key* (places), *the table* (dinners), *the index* (things worth spending on), *the receipt* (best €100 anyone spent).

### chain example — skincare
**01 · DESIRE** — moisturiser spilling over a peach.
**02 · THINKING** — why beauty increasingly looks edible.
**03 · MAKING** — if this launch were ours, the whole campaign is built on appetite: cream on fruit, skin macro, melting textures, breakfast tables, tasting-style creator reactions.

### the harvest rule
every THINKING post must be able to generate a MAKING post.

when you write *"brands are doing X because Y changed"*, immediately ask
*"okay — so what would we actually make?"* and write that down in `09 content bank`
before you publish. that is where next month's MAKING posts come from, and it is what
stops the account drifting into commentary.

## Before anyone makes anything

### the one-line brief
fill this in before a post is designed. five lines. no exceptions.

**layer:** DESIRE / THINKING / MAKING
**viewer should think:** ______
**single idea:** ______
**why undressed is the right person to say it:** ______
**what they should remember tomorrow:** ______

### the second table — feeling → format
the day-to-day planning tool. work backwards from the reaction.

| I want the viewer to think… | post this |
| --- | --- |
| I need to follow these people | a weird, beautiful visual with almost no explanation |
| they have incredible references | taste archive |
| they understand culture | an UNDRESSED cultural analysis |
| I've never thought about my category like that | BORROWED CODES |
| finally, social advice that isn't generic | a social principle explained through a real cultural shift |
| they see things before other agencies | interpreted trend / news |
| I wonder what they'd do with my company | IF IT WERE OURS |
| they can turn ideas into real creative | INSIGHT → EXECUTION |
| they have a proper strategic process | the undress / process post |
| they can actually produce this | BTS → final result |
| I should contact them | case study: problem → solution → result |
