# 08 · Tone of Voice

## What we sound like

### the five adjectives
**observant · specific · slightly provocative · culturally literate · short when possible**

short when possible is doing a lot of work. the strongest undressed lines are under
eight words.

### lowercase, always
matching the site and the CI. lowercase is not casual here — it's the refusal to shout
in the ordinary way. the shouting is done by gluten black at 120pt, not by capitals.

**the exceptions:** DESIRE / THINKING / MAKING and the series names (BORROWED CODES,
UNDRESSED, IF IT WERE OURS) stay uppercase. they read as stamps, not sentences.

### the existing taglines — still the spine
- truth has never looked this good.
- we see through you. that's a compliment.
- we strip what's fake. we build what's real.
- stop hiding. start working.
- what happens when brands go bare.
- the most powerful thing a brand can wear is nothing.

### the specificity rule
a number, a time, a place or a named thing beats an adjective every time.

| weak | undressed |
| --- | --- |
| a long discussion about art direction | three people argued about the shade of a napkin for 22 minutes |
| things went wrong on set | 11:37. everything we planned just became useless. |
| we plan carefully before shooting | the shoot starts three weeks before the camera comes out |
| a summer feeling | 6am bakeries, wet hair, white shirts, late lunches |

## What we never sound like

### the banned registers
- **snarky agency twitter** — being mean is not the same as being sharp
- **marketing guru** — "here's the framework that 10x'd our client's reach 🧵"
- **linkedin motivational** — "agree?" / "thoughts?" / "let that sink in"
- **fake cool** — slang we didn't grow up with, used to seem current
- **explainer voice** — "in today's fast-paced digital landscape"

### banned phrases — literally do not type these
storytelling · authentic · engaging content · scroll-stopping · game-changer ·
in today's digital landscape · unlock your potential · take it to the next level ·
elevate your brand · thought leadership · seamless · curated experience (used sincerely) ·
we're passionate about · at the end of the day · circle back · leverage · synergy ·
"drop a 🔥 if you agree" · "save this for later" · "which one are you?" ·
"here's what nobody tells you about…" · "hot take:" · "unpopular opinion:"

### also banned: the false-modesty opener
"we don't usually post about this, but…" / "I've been thinking a lot lately…"
just say the thing.

## Caption architecture

### the shapes
**DESIRE — four words or fewer.** the image argues. the caption stamps.
> wrong category. right feeling.

**THINKING — one paragraph that adds something the carousel didn't say.**
never a summary of the slides. if the caption repeats the post, delete it.

**MAKING — context, then the transposition.**
name the category, state the move, then leave the door open:
*nobody asked us. we did it anyway.*

### the CTA policy
no CTA on DESIRE. ever.
THINKING gets a question, and only when it's a real one — not "agree?"
MAKING gets an explicit invitation roughly one post in four, never every time.

the account sells. individual posts mostly don't. that distinction is the entire strategy.

### hashtags
five maximum, in the first comment, and only ones a human would plausibly follow.
no `#socialmediamarketing #contentcreator #agencylife`.

## The german rule

### what translates and what doesn't
same rule as the site (`js/data.jsx`): **headlines, taglines and series names stay english
in both languages.** that's the brand voice. body copy, explanatory slides and captions
translate.

so: `BORROWED CODES 04 — what can food steal from fashion?` stays english on the german
carousel. the four slides explaining it are german.

### why
switching the headline language breaks the recognisability the series depends on, and the
english lines are shorter, which is the whole point of the display type.

## The final check

### before it goes out
1. could another social media agency have posted this?
2. would I still care if the logo disappeared?
3. does it show taste or claim it?
4. is there an observation that makes someone think differently?
5. could a brand owner imagine their company becoming more interesting because of us?

### and the one that matters most
> if we removed the undressed logo, would this still feel like something only undressed
> would make or say?

if not, it isn't finished.
